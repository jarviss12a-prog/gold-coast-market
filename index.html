Our Story

More Than a Liquor Store. A River North Institution.

Nestled on Chicago Avenue in the heart of River North, Gold Coast Market has long been the neighborhood's go-to destination for quality spirits, craft beer, and everyday essentials. We know our regulars by name — and we're always happy to help a first-time visitor discover something new.

Whether you're stocking up before a dinner party, grabbing a bottle for the rooftop, or just need a late-night snack and a cold drink, we're here for it. Open every single day until midnight — because River North doesn't stop early, and neither do we.

  • Curated craft beer selection from local Chicago breweries and beyond
  • Rare and small-batch spirits from around the world
  • Knowledgeable staff who can help you find the perfect bottle
  • Competitive pricing with a neighborhood, non-chain feel
  • Convenience items, snacks, tobacco, and mixer essentials
